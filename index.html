GAN-balanced plant disease classification

Does generating minority-class images fix an imbalanced dataset?

A GAN generates extra leaf images for the under-represented classes of a 38-class plant disease dataset. A custom CNN is then trained on the balanced set and checked with 5-fold cross-validation.

HIGGS: logistic regression vs neural network

How much does model capacity matter at 11 million samples?

Both models were trained on 16 slices of the HIGGS collision dataset (11M events, 28 features), from 1% to 100%, to find where extra data stops paying off. The network's accuracy is already 76.6% at 30% of the data and only reaches 76.8% at 100%.
